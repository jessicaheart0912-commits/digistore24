Sound familiar?

You studied Korean. Then you walked into a Korean office.

Business Korean runs on three things nobody teaches in class.

존댓말
Honorifics

The same sentence changes depending on who you're talking to — and getting it wrong sounds rude.

호칭
Titles + 님

It's 김 부장님 — never a first name, never 당신. Titles change with every promotion.

눈치
Reading the room

"검토해 보겠습니다" ("we'll review it") often means… no. Learn to hear what isn't said.
