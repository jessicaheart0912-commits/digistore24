The course

Who is this course for?

Anyone working — or about to work — with Korean companies, clients or colleagues: new hires, expats, global team members, students and job seekers.

What level of Korean do I need?

Beginner to intermediate. If you can read Hangul, even slowly, you're ready. Every phrase includes romanization and an English translation.

What language is the course taught in?

English. Every Korean phrase is spoken slowly and clearly, twice.

How long is the course?

33 video lessons, about 66 minutes in total, across 4 parts and 22 chapters. Most students finish in 1–3 weeks.

Is there a book?

The course includes downloadable PDF slides, a phrase cheat sheet, email templates and the answer key from the companion workbook.

60-day money-back guarantee

If the course isn't right for you, you can request a full refund within 60 days of purchase. All orders and refunds are processed by Digistore24.

  1. Open the order confirmation email from Digistore24.
  2. Click the link to manage your order and choose “Request refund” — or contact Digistore24 support directly.
  3. Your refund is processed to your original payment method. Course access ends once the refund is issued.
